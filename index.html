Sanguine's OC Hub

Introduction

Hey! I'm Sanguine (or whatever) and this is my current hub of choice for all of my various sillies. It's very much a work in progress at the moment, so don't be surprised if you encounter a dead link. It also doesn't have many characters as of right now—they take a while to add!

The MP3 player code is from here!

The "Human Characters" section contains almost all of my human characters. There's a little bit of flexibility here; characters with supernatural abilities who are otherwise "human" will be sorted here, for example. This is likely to be the least populated category due to my preference for playing non-humans.

The "Non-Human Characters" section, believe it or not, contains almost all of my non-human characters. Vampires, original fantasy species, satyrs, centaurs, and the occasional anthropomorphic character all end up here. This section also includes characters who are half or even mostly human, but with some degree of non-humanity (like werewolves or quarter-vampires).

Last but not least, the "Exclusive Characters" section. This section is where all of the characters not in the above two sections end up! These are characters created for a specific roleplay or universe with a partner or partners. As you may have gleaned from the "Exclusive" part, I will not play these characters outside of their home universe/RP. The exclusivity of these characters is subject to change; for example, if a roleplay or universe is ended, I'll likely remove the exclusive status of the character and re-sort them into one of the other two sections.
